Section 1

1. Overview & Scope

Welcome to What2Eat ("What2Eat", "we", "our", or "us"). What2Eat is an AI-powered smart meal assistant designed to eliminate mealtime decision fatigue through recipe recommendations, weekly meal planning, fridge-ingredient matching, and local dining suggestions.

This Privacy Policy applies to the What2Eat Android application, our backend services, and any related web portals. By downloading, accessing, or using What2Eat, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to the practices described in this policy.

Section 2

2. Information We Collect

We only collect data necessary to provide a smooth, personalized cooking and dining experience:

A. Account Information (Voluntary Google Sign-In)

When you optionally choose to sign in with your Google Account, we receive profile information from Google Identity Services:

  • User Name & Email Address: Used to identify your account and sync saved recipes across devices.
  • Profile Picture URL: Displayed within your personal app drawer header.
  • Unique Firebase UID: A secure identifier assigned to your account in our database.

B. Guest / Anonymous Mode

If you use What2Eat without signing in, we generate a localized, anonymous device identifier so you can still save favorites, track weekly meals, and access features without providing any personal identity.

C. Preferences & Meal Data

  • Dietary preferences (e.g., vegetarian, keto, halal, quick meals).
  • Pantry and fridge ingredients you input for recipe generation.
  • Saved weekly meal plans, grocery checklists, and favorite dishes.

D. Location Information (City & Country)

To suggest local cuisine and nearby dining options in the Dine Out feature, What2Eat allows you to select your country and city from our dashboard. We do not track your precise GPS coordinates in real-time in the background.

💡
Location Privacy Notice:

Your selected city and country are used strictly to provide localized food recommendations and regional meal ideas. They are never sold, rented, or shared with advertisers.

E. Diagnostic & Telemetry Data

  • Firebase Analytics: Aggregated anonymous usage metrics (e.g., which feature is used: Meal Decider, Weekly Planner, Dine Out) to help us optimize the app.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Crash logs, device model, Android OS version, and stack traces to fix bugs promptly.
Section 3

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following specific purposes:

  • Generating Personalized Recipes: Tailoring AI-assisted meal recipes to your selected ingredients, dietary constraints, and regional taste.
  • Weekly Meal Synchronization: Syncing your meal calendars and grocery shopping items across your signed-in devices via Google Cloud Firestore.
  • Subscription Entitlement: Verifying your What2Eat Pro / VIP status to unlock unlimited AI suggestions, premium recipes, and ad-free experience.
  • App Stability: Monitoring performance, preventing crashes, and resolving software defects.
Section 4

4. Third-Party Services & Processors

What2Eat works with trusted industry-leading infrastructure providers compliant with global privacy standards:

  • Google Play Services & Google Identity: Provides authentication, security, and core Android runtime services. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Firebase (Firestore, Analytics, Crashlytics): Secure cloud database, crash diagnostics, and anonymous telemetry. Firebase Privacy Info.
  • Google Gemini / Generative AI APIs: Processes ingredient and recipe prompts to generate meal suggestions. Prompts do not contain your personal passwords or payment data.
Section 5

5. Subscriptions & Payments

What2Eat offers optional in-app subscriptions and lifetime purchases (What2Eat Pro / VIP).

🔒
100% Secure Google Play Billing:

All financial transactions are handled exclusively by Google Play In-App Billing. What2Eat never collects, stores, or processes your credit card, debit card, bank account, or CVV numbers.

Section 6

6. Data Security & Storage

We employ strict technical and organizational measures to safeguard your personal data:

  • All network communications between the app and our servers use encrypted TLS/HTTPS protocols.
  • Cloud database records stored in Google Cloud Firestore are protected with enterprise-grade encryption at rest and role-based security rules.
  • We do not sell, barter, or monetize your personal information to third-party data brokers or advertising networks.
Section 7

7. Your Rights & Account Deletion

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as the European Union under GDPR, or California under CCPA), you have the right to access, rectify, or delete your data.

To delete your account and all associated cloud data, you can tap Sign Out within the app, or email our support desk at fawwadshamim@gmail.com with your User ID. We will purge your records within 30 days.

Section 8

8. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

What2Eat does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). If we become aware that we have collected personal data from a child without parental consent, we will take immediate steps to delete that data.

Section 9

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new features, legal regulations, or technical improvements. Any modifications will be posted directly to this web page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
